QuoteWell Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

QuoteWell is a quote and invoice app for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. QuoteWell does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. We have no servers.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter, including your business details, logo, customers, saved items, quotes and invoices, is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

PDFs you share are handed to the app you choose (Mail, Messages, Files and so on) using the standard iOS share sheet. Those apps are governed by their own privacy policies.

Purchases

QuoteWell Pro is sold through Apple's App Store using Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Analytics and tracking

QuoteWell contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.

Children

QuoteWell is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
