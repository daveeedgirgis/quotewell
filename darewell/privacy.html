DareWell Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 September 2026

DareWell is made by David Girgis. This policy explains what the app stores and where it goes.

What the app stores

The game title and challenges the organiser writes, plus the notes, timestamps and photos players attach when they mark a challenge done, and the display name you choose for yourself. This information is stored on your device.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your games are stored in your own private iCloud database so they sync between your devices. When a game is shared, the game, its players and its completions are shared with the people who join it through Apple's iCloud sharing, and they can see and add to the shared information. The organiser can stop sharing or remove a player at any time from the game's Info screen. Apple's iCloud terms apply to that storage.

Camera and photos

DareWell asks for camera access only when you attach a photo to a completed challenge, and for photo library access only when you choose an existing photo instead. Photos are stored with the game only; nothing is uploaded to us.

What we do not do

DareWell has no accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect analytics, do not use tracking, do not show advertising and never see the information you enter.

Purchases

DareWell Unlimited is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. It is not a subscription. We receive no payment details.

Children

DareWell is a party game for friends and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete or leave a game inside the app to remove it from your device and, if you use iCloud, from your iCloud database. Deleting the app removes everything stored locally.
