Dare Bingo Support

Challenge cards with friends

Dare Bingo turns a list of challenges into a shared bingo card for a group of friends. The organiser builds the card and invites players with a link or a QR code; everyone marks their squares done with a note and, when a square asks for it, a photo, and watches a live leaderboard. It works with no account, and players join a game through an iCloud share link.

Frequently asked questions

What is free?

Games with up to 10 players are free, with unlimited challenges, photos and completions. Dare Bingo Unlimited is a one-time purchase (USD 4.99) that removes the player cap on every game you organise. There is no subscription.

How do friends join a game?

Open Invite in the game and share the link or QR code with Messages, Mail or any app. When someone opens it on their iPhone or iPad, the game appears in their Dare Bingo and their marks sync with everyone else. Joining needs the player to be signed in to iCloud.

Does Dare Bingo need a connection?

Your own marks are saved on your device immediately. Other players' marks usually appear within a minute once you have a connection; there is no manual sync button because none is needed.

How do I mark a challenge complete?

Tap an open square, add a note and, if that square requires one, a photo from the camera or your photo library, then tap Mark complete. You can undo your own mark for 60 seconds afterwards.

What happens if a completion looks wrong?

Any player can flag a completion they think is unfair. The organiser sees flagged completions under Review and can reject or restore them. Flags are only visible to the organiser and the player who raised them.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Dare Bingo, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
